# 1-Minute Elevator Pitch (120 Words)

**Project Title**: AI-Based Driver Drowsiness & Safety Monitoring System  

---

> "Driver drowsiness causes severe industrial transportation accidents, yet existing commercial monitors merely emit passive alarms that drivers habituate to. To solve this, we built a real-time deep learning prototype combining a MobileNetV2 CNN feature extractor with a 64-unit LSTM temporal network processing 16-frame video sequences at 78.65 ms latency. 
> 
> Our core novelty is a **Closed-Loop Intervention & Automated Recovery Verification Workflow** ($\text{DETECT} \rightarrow \text{INTERVENE} \rightarrow \text{VERIFY} \rightarrow \text{RESUME/ESCALATE}$). The system issues warnings, verifies driver wakefulness ('look at camera & keep eyes open'), and enforces simulated rest protocols before resuming monitoring. 
> 
> Experimentally, we achieved a 0.9479 Validation ROC-AUC, 100% NITYMED recall, and identified critical cross-dataset illumination domain shift. Our next step is multi-modal feature fusion with infrared cameras."
